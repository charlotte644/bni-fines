    How the white elephant swap works

    1. Bring one wrapped gift, unisex and worth no more than £10, and add it to the pile at the front.
    2. Your turn comes in the order of your number on the BNI meeting sheet, so number 1 goes first.
    3. On your turn, choose: take a wrapped gift from the pile, or steal a wrapped gift from someone who already has one.
    4. If your gift is stolen, take a new one from the pile or steal from someone else. You can't steal straight back the gift that was just taken from you.
    5. Each gift can only be stolen twice. After the second steal it stays with its new owner.
    6. Keep it wrapped! Nobody opens anything until everyone has a gift.
    7. On the count of three, everyone opens their gift at the same time, so nobody knows who's got what until the very end.

    Top tip: the best gifts are fun or useful. Think festive treats, a quirky mug, or something every business owner needs.
