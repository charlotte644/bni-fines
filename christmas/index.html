🐘White elephant swap

A gift swap with a cheeky twist: you might not keep the gift you open! The full rules are below.

🐘How the white elephant swap works

  1. Bring one wrapped gift, unisex and worth no more than £10, and add it to the pile at the front.
  2. Everyone draws a number to decide the order.
  3. Number 1 opens a gift from the pile and shows it to everyone.
  4. Everyone after that has a choice: open a new gift from the pile, or steal a gift someone has already opened.
  5. If your gift is stolen, you can open a new gift or steal from someone else. You can't steal straight back the gift that was just taken from you.
  6. Each gift can only be stolen twice. After the second steal it's frozen and stays with its new owner.
  7. The game ends when the last gift is opened. Then number 1 gets one final chance to swap with anyone.

Top tip: the best gifts are fun or useful. Think festive treats, a quirky mug, or something every business owner needs.
